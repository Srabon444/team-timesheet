Privacy Policy — Daily Timesheet Auto-Filler

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Daily Timesheet Auto-Filler ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that tracks time per project and fills your team's Fillout timesheet form. It is unofficial and not affiliated with Fillout.

Data the extension handles

DataWhere it livesWhy
Your name (as picked on the timesheet form), timer entries (project, category, description, time), settings, activity logchrome.storage.local on your deviceThe core timesheet feature
City, country and calculation settings for prayer reminders (only if you turn the feature on)chrome.storage.localPrayer-time reminders
Your Google account's access token (only if you click Connect)Managed by Chrome (chrome.identity); never stored by the extension's own code, never sent anywhere except GoogleOptional Google Drive backup/sync

The extension has no server of its own. The developer never receives your data.

Network requests

  • techzu.fillout.com — the extension reads the form's name/project/category lists and fills the form you have open. Your entries go to the form only when you start the fill, and you review the form before its final Submit.
  • Google Drive API (googleapis.com, oauth2.googleapis.com) — only after you click Connect. The extension requests the drive.file scope, which lets it see only the files it creates itself in your Drive, never your other files. It writes your timesheet data there as backup/sync files. "Disconnect" revokes the token.
  • api.aladhan.com — only if you enable prayer reminders. The city, country and calculation-method values you entered are sent to fetch prayer times.

No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry. No data is sold or shared with third parties, and none is used for creditworthiness, lending or any purpose unrelated to the features above. The extension does not load or run remote code.

Your control

  • Delete all local data by removing the extension or using Reset in Settings.
  • Delete Drive backups by deleting the extension's files in your Google Drive, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data

The extension's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/Srabon444/team-timesheet/issues.
